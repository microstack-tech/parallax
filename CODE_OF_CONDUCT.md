# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Parallax is built on the principles of neutrality, openness, and decentralization. We expect the same from our community.

**Expected behavior:**

- Constructive technical discussion focused on the project
- Respectful disagreement — challenge ideas, not people
- Clear, good-faith communication

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or trolling
- Publishing others' private information

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channels associated with Parallax.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from the project.

## Reporting

Report issues to the maintainers via GitHub or the project's communication channels. All reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.
